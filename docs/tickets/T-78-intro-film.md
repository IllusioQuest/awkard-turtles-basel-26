# T-78: Intro film, Bob's house to the Basel map

- **Owner:** Claude · **Status:** in progress · **Branch:** `feat/T-78-intro-film`
- **Depends on:** T-06 (reference-house figures), T-67 (Dreispitz pool scenario), T-21 (storyboard).

A roughly 110-second intro film rendered in Blender 4.x, built only from Python scripts in
`video/blender/` (no add-ons, no downloaded assets). Story: Bob is cold on a January evening in
Basel and opens his heating bill; a data centre nearby releases heat into the night; the camera
dives into Bob's basement, where four servers wake up and heat the house; weeks pass until the
tank is full and the servers pause; the year's key figures appear; the camera rises out of the street
onto the Basel map, where a 2025 time-lapse shows the work moving to an indoor pool in summer. The live
demo follows; the globe zoom-out after the demo is not part of this ticket.

Numbers come only from the repository and are kept with their source in `video/blender/numbers.json`
(generated by `video/blender/prepare_data.py`). Timing lives in `video/blender/timeline.json` and is
synced to `docs/pitch/voiceover.md` once that exists. How to run: `video/blender/README.md`.
