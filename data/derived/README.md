# Derived data

Files here are produced by scripts from `data/raw/` and are never edited by hand.

For every derived file, document below: the producing command, its inputs, and whether it is committed.
Commit a derived file when the demo, the dashboard or the jury needs to see it without running
anything (e.g. the yearly results of the reference house). Otherwise it is git-ignored and rebuilt.

| File | Produced by | Inputs | Committed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reference-house/*.json` (five server-count comparisons) | `python scripts/export_reference_house.py` (.NET 8 required) | Basel 2025 hourly weather, ElCom 2026 snapshot, `config/reference-house.json`, `config/reference-house-results.json`, `config/job-scenarios/unlimited.json`, WP2 `data/raw/market/costs.csv`; script invokes the shared C# `DigitalHeat.YearRun` | yes; compact draft/jury figures |
| `reference-house/transcription-basel-finite-only.json` | `python scripts/export_reference_house.py` (.NET 8 required) | Same house, weather and price, plus the sourced finite `config/job-scenarios/transcription-basel.json`; invokes the same C# `DigitalHeat.YearRun` | yes; diagnostic missed-deadline rate, separate from the unlimited-work comparison |
| `computing-price.json` | `python3 scripts/fetch_data.py --prices` (new snapshot) or `python3 scripts/fetch_prices.py --snapshot <file>` (pinned, offline) | latest snapshot in `data/raw/market/price-snapshots/` (path + SHA-256 recorded in the file), `config/computing-price-method.json`, `ServerElectricKw` from `config/reference-house.json`, RunPod rows of `data/raw/market/compute-prices.csv` | yes; derived market-tracking price (C1) and A1/A2, **not** the adopted scored value (that stays in `config/reference-house.json`, T-06) |
| `profitability/summary.json` | `python3 scripts/profitability_inputs.py` | `data/raw/market/*.csv` (T-37 rows), `config/hardware/*.json`, `reference-house/explorer-default.json` | yes; tables of `docs/research/profitability-inputs.md` |
| `network/experiments/*.csv`, `*.jsonl` (T-12 experiments A–D; `-lp` files add LP runs) | `python3 scripts/network_experiments.py [--lp --jobs N]` (.NET 8) | `config/network/switzerland-test.json` (synthetic test network), weather, `config/reference-house.json`; strategies of decisions 0025/0029 | yes; small, needed for the paper and the jury (labelled scenario) |
| `demand/*.csv` (T-51 demand shapes, job durations, GPU price history, `summary.csv`) | `python3 -I scripts/fetch_demand_traces.py` (add `--no-download` to reuse `data/external/`) | Azure LLM 2024, BurstGPT, Alibaba GPU 2020 task table (downloaded to `data/external/`), `data/raw/demand-traces/getdeploying-gpu-prices/weekly-2026-11-21.csv` | yes; small (~120 KB), read in `docs/research/compute-demand-traces.md` |
| `elcom/price-per-municipality-2026.csv` (T-51; H1–H8 × 2,123 municipalities, CHF/kWh) | `python3 -I scripts/fetch_elcom_municipalities.py --csv` (omit `--csv` to refetch) | `data/raw/elcom/municipalities-2026/*.json` | yes; ~1.4 MB |
| `buildings/municipality-buildings-heat.csv`, `buildings/grid-10km-heat-demand.csv` (T-51; building sizes and modelled heat demand) | `python3 -I scripts/build_buildings_heat.py --download` (omit `--download` to reuse `data/external/`) | GWR `ch.zip` and BFE heat demand GeoPackage (both in `data/external/`, not committed), `data/raw/swiss-grid/grid-10km.csv`; method in `data/raw/buildings/SOURCE.md` | yes; ~1.8 MB |
| `price_comparison.csv` (T-16: our cost per unit vs. market prices) | `python3 scripts/price_comparison.py` | `config/price-comparison.json`, `config/hardware/*.json`, `config/reference-house.json`, `data/raw/market/ai-services.csv` | yes; small, used by the pitch and the paper |
| `live-demo/recording.jsonl` (recorded fallback frames) | `python scripts/record_live_demo.py --minutes 2` while LiveGrid is running | local `/api/state` responses sampled every 0.4 seconds; captured 2026-11-21 on the demo laptop | yes; 300 frames, ~311 KB, replayed by `DigitalHeat.LiveGrid -- --replay` |
