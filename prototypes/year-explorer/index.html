Every assumption

Simplifications of this prototype: hot water is spread evenly over 24 h (the case gives only 12 kWh/day); the scheduler only sees the current hour (no forecast); job demand is unlimited, so missed deadlines are not modelled yet; the tank starts empty on 1 January.
