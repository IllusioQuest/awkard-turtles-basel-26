How to read it

Each panel is a house with one server. The slider sets the room temperature the house wants (an illustrative input, not a sensor); higher means more heat wanted this hour, and the room colour follows it. Every hour (a few seconds here) the network scheduler sets each server's load from that heat and moves waiting tasks whose house cannot finish them before their deadline.

Big circle in the middle: the task running now (ring = measured CPU load; pale = paused). Small circles: waiting tasks. Number = job, colour = house the job came from. 1 task ≈ 1 server-hour of real Ising Monte Carlo in a Docker container.

Keys: 1–4 new job in that house · S switch scheduler · A auto-spawn · R reset · ? this help
